## 👋 About Azum AI

**Azum AI** is an AI-focused agency turning ambitious ideas into practical, production-ready AI solutions. We focus on creating useful technology that helps businesses streamline workflows, make better use of information, and build smarter digital products.

Our goal is simple: **make advanced AI useful in the real world.**

| Focus | How we help |
| --- | --- |
| **🤖 AI Agents & Assistants** | Build intelligent agents and assistants that support people and business processes. |
| **🧠 LLM-Powered Applications** | Turn large language models into useful, purpose-built product experiences. |
| **⚡ Intelligent Automation** | Connect tools and automate repetitive workflows with AI where it adds value. |
| **🚀 AI Product Development** | Take AI product ideas from concept through implementation and iteration. |
